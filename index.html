01 / THE PLACEMORE THAN A COFFEE STOP

A place with
room for ideas.

Some afternoons call for a drink and a catch-up. Other nights call for a gig you never knew you needed.

Café Blah is an independent, community-focused café, bar, gallery, music venue and cinema on Wilmslow Road. Local art, people and events are part of the place—not extras added to a coffee menu.

It reopened at its current address in 2025 after the community rallied around the venue. Now it keeps making space for Withington to get together.
