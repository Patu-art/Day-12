# Plere / Day 12 — mobile photo brief

Please upload original files here in the ChatGPT conversation (no ZIP, renaming, editing, or desktop needed). These should be photographs of **Plere at 48 Beech Road**, not another Chorlton pub or generic stock imagery.

## Priority (three photos are enough to start)
1. HERO: one wide, atmospheric photo of the actual bar's interior with seating, bar counter and/or vintage posters; ideally landscape, bright enough to show the venue.
2. EXTERIOR: one clear photo of the actual Plere shopfront/sign and/or terrace on Beech Road, preferably landscape.
3. FOOD / DRINK: one well-lit table scene with Plere's actual drinks or small plates (not an invented menu item).

## Optional, for a richer finished website
4. One close-up of the actual taps, draft beer or wine served there.
5. One photograph of the real small plates / cheese / charcuterie at the venue.
6. One shot of the actual vinyl decks, DJ or vintage-poster wall, with people photographed only where their publication is permitted.

## Format
Send the largest-resolution originals available; ideally at least 1600px wide for the hero and 900px for supplementary photos. JPG, PNG and WEBP are fine. Avoid screenshots containing the Instagram interface, watermarks or overlaid captions when originals are available. Mixed portrait and landscape crops are useful. Prefer natural light and genuine venue photographs over AI-generated stock.

## Permission
The business's public Instagram and press photography is a good place to **identify** suitable images, but public availability does not automatically grant reuse rights. Screenshots can guide the design and private pitch, but obtain permission from the photographer/venue before embedding photos in a publicly hosted, client-facing demo or handing the site to the business. Do not remove watermarks or crop away credits. If permission is uncertain, keep original illustrative artwork and link to the official gallery instead.

## Source of current images
No real photographs have yet been supplied for this Plere build. The hero, drinks and food graphics are original CSS illustrations, not depictions of actual venue interiors or dishes.
